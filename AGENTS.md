## Explanation preferences

- I want AI responses that are clear, structured, and accurate. I want enough detail to make good decisions, but not so much that it becomes overwhelming. When I’m learning something new, I want options and implications laid out. When I’m refining work, I prefer short, focused summaries that cut out noise. I want insight I can act on, not complexity for its own sake.

## What AI may draft

- The assistant may draft early versions of briefs, outlines, summaries, templates, and structured starting points for analyses or capability documents. It can help organize ideas, frame options, and create scaffolds that I will refine.
- I will write all final recommendations, interpretations of data, regulatory or safety assessments, and anything that reflects organizational strategy or professional judgment. Anything involving real operations, internal processes, or sensitive context must come from me, not the assistant.

## Content never to paste into a model

- No patient‑identifiable information, protected health information (PHI), internal audit findings, incident reports, or any regulatory‑sensitive material will be entered into this model or stored anywhere in this repository.

## Review expectations

I verify all AI‑generated content for accuracy, safety, and correctness before committing it to this repository. Specifically, I check:
- Numbers and calculations — ensuring they are correct, reproducible, and based on valid assumptions.
- Sources and references — confirming they are legitimate, relevant, and not fabricated.
- Regulatory and safety alignment — confirming that nothing includes PHI, patient‑identifiable information, internal audit findings, incident reports, or any sensitive operational material.
- Fit and intent — ensuring the draft matches my voice, purpose, and the standards of quality, patient safety, and regulatory practice.
