# Perfect Competition — Engagement Brief

The farm has 64 beds and must choose how many beds of tomatoes, carrots, and mesclun to plant for a 36‑week season. Because prices are set entirely by the farmers’ market, the farm is a price taker and cannot influence crop prices. With price fixed, the decision reduces to quantity: plant beds until the marginal cost of the next bed rises to meet the market price (P = MC).
